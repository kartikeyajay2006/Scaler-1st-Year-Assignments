Maggi Recipe

  1. Take 1 cup of water in a pan.
  2. Boil the water.
  3. Add Maggi noodles and tastemaker (spice mix).
  4. Cook for 2-3 minutes.
  5. Stir sometimes so it doesn’t stick.
  6. Once noodles are soft, turn off the gas.
  7. Done! Eat hot.

Tea Recipe

  1. Take 1 cup of water in a pan.
  2. Add 1 spoon tea leaves (chai patti).
  3. Add sugar as you like.
  4. Boil it for 2-3 minutes.
  5. Add half cup of milk.
  6. Boil again for 2-3 minutes.
  7. Strain it in a cup.
  8. Done! Your tea is ready.
